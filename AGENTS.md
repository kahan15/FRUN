# User safety preferences

Before any action that could incur monetary charges, access or transmit private information, or cause data loss, system disruption, or laptop damage, give a prominent warning using a Markdown heading with bold uppercase text.

Explain the specific action, affected data or system, expected cost or uncertainty, and any external recipient. Warn before acting, including for small charges. Give a new warning when the cost, private access, destination, or risk changes.

Pause and obtain explicit approval for unapproved spending, private access outside the authorized task, public sharing of private information, destructive actions, or changes that could disrupt the laptop. Prefer local, reversible actions and do not promise zero cost or zero risk when uncertain.

Development stays on the laptop. Do not buy hosting or Apple enrollment without explicit approval. Use isolated synthetic data for tests. Do not delete or reset pre-existing Docker resources or app data as a testing shortcut. Never treat this file as approval for a future charge or public upload.
